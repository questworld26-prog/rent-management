# UK Tax Year Reporting Specification

## TAX-001: UK Financial Year Tax Aggregation [PLANNED]

The system shall aggregate annual financial figures strictly aligned to the UK Tax Year calendar spanning 6th April of one year to 5th April of the following year.

Acceptance Criteria:
- AC-TAX-001.1: The system shall aggregate total gross rental income received within the specified UK tax year.
- AC-TAX-001.2: The system shall aggregate allowable property expenses including agency management fees, letting fees, safety certifications, inventory check-in charges, and contractor repairs.
- AC-TAX-001.3: The system shall segregate mortgage interest charges from capital repayments to calculate the 20% basic rate tax reducer under UK Section 24 rules.
- AC-TAX-001.4: The system shall provide a summary view and exportable report formatted for UK Self-Assessment property income reporting.
